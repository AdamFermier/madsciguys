A pharma product developer coordinates research, formulation, manufacturing, and regulatory strategy to bring new therapies to market. This persona is a key connector between science, engineering, design, and human-centered outcomes.

In the STEAM knowledge graph, the pharma product developer traverses all five domains: grounded in **Science** (chemistry, biology), structured by **Arts** (knowledge management, QbD), validated by **Mathematics** (predictive modeling), implemented in **Engineering** (manufacturing), and enabled by **Technology** (digital tools, Industry 4.0).
