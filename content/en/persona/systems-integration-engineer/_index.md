A systems integration engineer connects sensors, control systems, software, and people to make intelligent manufacturing and smart systems work together. This persona blends engineering rigor with systems thinking and collaborative design.

In the STEAM knowledge graph, the systems integration engineer lives at the boundary of **Technology** and **Engineering**, traversing `implements`, `standardized-by`, and `enables` edges as they wire together the physical and digital layers of Industry 4.0 environments.
