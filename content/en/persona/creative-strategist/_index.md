A creative strategist helps teams translate insights into experiences, using storytelling, design, and systems thinking to connect STEAM disciplines.

In the STEAM knowledge graph, the creative strategist operates primarily in the **Arts** domain — but their edges run in every direction: shaping the UX of **Technology**, framing questions for **Science**, and formalizing findings through **Mathematics**. They are the connective tissue of the STEAM graph.
