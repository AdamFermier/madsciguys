An analytical chemist ensures data quality, traceability, and measurement accuracy across scientific and engineering projects. This persona is a natural bridge between laboratory science and creative innovation.

In the STEAM knowledge graph, the analytical chemist sits at the intersection of **Science** (Chemistry) and **Arts** (Pharma, Control Strategy), generating the empirical data that flows into **Mathematics** (Predictive Modeling) and **Technology** (Big Data analytics).
