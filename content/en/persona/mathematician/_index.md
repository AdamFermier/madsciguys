A mathematician brings analytical rigor, modelling, and statistical insight to interdisciplinary teams. In STEAM, mathematicians collaborate with designers, chemists, and engineers to turn abstract models into real-world solutions.

In the STEAM knowledge graph, the mathematician is anchored in **Mathematics** and their primary edge is `models` — they take phenomena from **Science** and formalize them, then feed optimized outputs back to **Technology** and **Engineering**. They also give semantic rigor to **Arts** through the `formalized by` relationship.
