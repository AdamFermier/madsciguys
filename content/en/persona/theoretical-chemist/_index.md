A theoretical chemist uses mathematics, physics, and computing to model molecules and reactions. This persona bridges chemistry, math, and engineering by creating insights that shape experiments, materials, and new technologies.

In the STEAM knowledge graph, the theoretical chemist sits at the overlap of **Science** (chemistry, physics) and **Mathematics** (predictive modeling, simulation), traversing `models`, `grounded-in`, and `formalizes` edges to translate quantum-level phenomena into actionable scientific knowledge.
